About South Wales

Trusted Scaffolding Services Across South Wales

We're a locally owned and operated scaffolding company providing safe, reliable and professional scaffolding solutions across South Wales. With a strong commitment to quality workmanship, safety and customer satisfaction, we support homeowners, contractors and businesses with scaffolding services tailored to their project requirements.

We specialise in domestic scaffold, roof access scaffold, narrow access scaffold, and towers and temporary structures scaffold, providing practical access solutions for roofing repairs, property renovations, extensions, maintenance work and construction projects. Whether you need scaffolding for a small domestic property or a more complex project, our team works hard to deliver a safe, secure and efficient service from start to finish.

Our experienced team understands that every property and project is different. That's why we focus on clear communication, dependable scheduling and carefully planned scaffolding installations. We take safety seriously, follow relevant industry standards and aim to minimise disruption to your home or business throughout the process.

We proudly provide scaffolding services throughout South Wales, including Cardiff, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport, Dinas Powys, Taff's Well, Rogerstone, Bedwas, Abertridwr, Senghenydd, Church Village, Pontyclun, Llanbradach, Beddau, Ystrad Mynach, Llantrisant, Talbot Green, Llanharan, Brynna, Cwmbran, Caerleon, Pencoed, Llanharry, Abercynon, Nelson, Bargoed, Pontllanfraith, Newbridge, Abercarn, Risca, Treharris and Oakdale.

At our company, we believe that professional scaffolding is about more than simply providing access. It's about delivering peace of mind, maintaining high safety standards and giving every customer a service they can rely on.
